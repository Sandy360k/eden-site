Common questions

Where is my data? In your own Eden account (Supabase, Mumbai region), visible only to you. See the privacy policy.

Does Eden use AI? Yes, to split and sort what you say and to answer questions, and only after you allow it. Turn it off any time in Settings → Your data → Share with Eden’s AI.

How do I get my data out? Settings → Your data → Export (Markdown or JSON).

How do I delete my account? Settings → Profile and account → Delete account. It removes your account and everything in it at once. No phone? See Delete your Eden account.
