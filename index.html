[01] The split · TP-02 thermal

One sentence in. A receipt of Seeds out.

Say it the way it comes out. Eden splits it into small Seeds, one line each, stamped with what it is, when, where and which part of your life.

“Remind me to send Ravi the site photos tonight, and Meera’s birthday is on Monday.”

  • WHENA time or a day, if you said one. Eden never invents a deadline.
  • WHEREA place you saved, like the shop or the office: it comes back when you’re there.
  • AREAWork, Home, Family, Ideas: the colour on the stamp.

[02] How it works · Drawing EDN-02 · Scale 1:1

From one sentence to the right reminder.

No forms, no folders. Four stages, and you only do the first.

  1. Fig. 01

    Capture

    Hold the orb and speak, or type. It’s saved on your phone first, so nothing is lost offline.

    In
    Voice or text
    Offline
    Saved, synced later

  2. Fig. 02

    Split

    One sentence becomes as many Seeds as it holds, each with its type, its day and its place.

    Out
    1 → n Seeds
    Type
    Task · event · note · idea

  3. Fig. 03

    Link

    Each Seed joins the people, places and projects it’s about, so it never floats on its own.

    Links
    People · places · projects
    Stored
    By meaning

  4. Fig. 04

    Resurface

    At the right time, or when you arrive at the right place, Eden brings it back. Nothing to dig for.

    When
    A time or a place
    Places
    Checked on your phone

Gauge 01 · Confidence

Eden never acts on a guess.

Every Seed carries how sure Eden is. That number decides what happens next.

  • < 0.60Asks you. “Is that Thursday this week or next?”
  • 0.60–0.85Acts, with undo, and says “Eden thinks…”
  • > 0.85Just does it. You can still edit or undo.

Features · in Eden today

What Eden does for you.

Talk or type

One tap on the orb. Hold it for a brain dump: talk for minutes, pauses are fine.

Nothing gets lost

Every capture is saved on your phone first and synced later, even offline.

The right place

Save home, office or your shops. Eden reminds you of the right Seeds when you arrive. Where you are never leaves your phone.

The right time

Reminders when things are due, and your calendar in the same view.

Eden chat

A hello that tells you what’s due today and what slipped, then talk or type to Eden.

Ask Eden

Ask about anything you said. Every answer shows the Seeds it came from.

Share to Eden

Share text or a screenshot from any app, and its text becomes a capture.

Projects

Steps and progress, built from what you said.

Your household

Share Seeds with the people you live with.

Asks before it guesses

When Eden isn’t sure, it asks. Anything it does on its own can be undone.

Every device

iPhone, Android, the web, and Windows and Mac with Ctrl Shift E from anywhere.

Yours to keep

Lock Eden with Face ID, export everything, or delete your account from the app.

[06] Privacy, in plain words

Your second brain is yours.

The full text is short too: privacy policy and terms of use.

  • Only you can see your Seeds.Unless you choose to share one with your Home.
  • Eden doesn’t track where you go.Place alerts run on your phone, for the places you saved; where you are isn’t sent.
  • You decide about AI.Eden’s AI reads what you say only after you allow it, and you can turn that off any time.
  • No ads, no selling, no tracking across apps.Not now, not later. This page has no trackers either.
  • Take it with you, or delete it.Export everything as Markdown or JSON, or delete your account from the app in one step.
