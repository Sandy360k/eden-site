How it works

From one sentence to the right reminder.

No forms, no folders. Say it the way it comes out; Eden does the sorting.

  1. “Remind me to send Ravi the site photos tonight, and Meera’s birthday is on Monday.”

    You talk

    Hold the orb and speak, or type. One go, as long or short as you like.

  2. Send Ravi the site photos
    Meera’s birthday

    It becomes Seeds

    Eden splits what you said into small units of meaning, each with what it is: a task, an event, an idea.

  3. Ravi House renovation Meera Family Home

    Linked to your life

    Each Seed joins the people, places and projects it is about, so it never floats on its own.

  4. Tonight, 8 pmSend Ravi the site photos
    MondayMeera’s birthday

    Back at the right moment

    At the right time, or when you arrive at the right place, Eden brings it back. Nothing to dig for.

The Seed Garden

Everything you said, linked in time.

Every Seed finds its place: on a day, in a project, beside the ones it belongs with.

  • SeedsA list, your day, or the Garden, where linked Seeds grow along the same vine.
  • CalendarDay, week and month: what is due, and when.
  • ProjectsSteps and progress, built from what you said: “New substation: 4 of 7 steps done.”
  • Ask“How far is the substation project?” Eden answers from your own Seeds.

Talk or type

Every capture is saved on your phone first and synced later, even offline.

Save home, office or your shops. Eden reminds you of the right Seeds when you arrive. Where you are never leaves your phone.

The right time

Reminders when things are due, and your calendar in the same view.

Eden chat

Ask Eden

Ask about anything you said. Every answer shows the Seeds it came from.

Share to Eden

Projects

Your household

Asks before it guesses

Privacy, in plain words

Your second brain is yours.

The full text is short too: privacy policy and terms of use.

  • Only you can see your Seeds.Unless you choose to share one with your Home.
  • Eden doesn’t track where you go.Place alerts run on your phone, for the places you saved; where you are isn’t sent.
  • You decide about AI.Eden’s AI reads what you say only after you allow it, and you can turn that off any time.
  • No ads, no selling, no tracking across apps.Not now, not later. This page has no trackers either.
  • Take it with you, or delete it.Export everything as Markdown or JSON, or delete your account from the app in one step.
